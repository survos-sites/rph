# CHRISTMAS, FACING WEST: Teaser, revised script (about 71 seconds)

**Cast:** JESS (late 20s), BEN (about 30), LINDA (Jess's mother, late 50s), DOT (70s), PA VOLUNTEER, CROWD.

**Music:** An original piano theme, a rising five-note figure. Only four notes ever play until the last three seconds.

**On-screen text:** Three cards only: ANNVILLE, PENNSYLVANIA · CHRISTMAS, FACING WEST · COMING THIS CHRISTMAS.

**Picture rule:** Never show a face in close-up while that character is speaking. Play the line over whoever is listening, over an object, or over a silhouette.

**The arc:** She refuses Christmas. She's forced home. She's forced to stay. She chooses to stay.

---

## STAGE 1: She refuses, then she has to come home

### 0:00–0:09 · "That's for me"
- **PICTURE:** Black for 2 seconds. Then Jess at her Manhattan desk at 9 p.m. Her window faces a brick airshaft lit by someone else's fluorescent light. Slow push in.
- **SOUND:** HVAC drone, keyboard, phone-line hiss. No music.
- **DIALOGUE:**
  - JESS *(fast, still typing, apologizing in advance)*: "Mom, I can't do Christmas this year. If I leave, a guy named Tyler gets my accounts."
  - LINDA *(on the phone; warm, unhurried; she has never once raised her voice to win)*: "No, I know. I know."
  - *(A siren rises on Linda's end only, squeezed into the phone's narrow range)*
  - JESS *(typing stops)*: "…What is that?"
  - LINDA *(pleasant, as if it's the mailman)*: "Hm? Oh. That's for me."
- **TRANSITION:** The siren bends down in pitch and becomes the theme's first piano note.

### 0:09–0:13 · The deal
- **PICTURE:** The Tesla on Route 422 at night, sleet in the headlights. Inside, Jess grips the wheel, still in her office clothes.
- **SOUND:** Sparse piano. Her side of a call over the car speaker; the other voice is only a faint murmur.
- **DIALOGUE:**
  - JESS *(clipped, negotiating, hiding the panic)*: "Two days. Tyler doesn't touch anything."

### 0:13–0:20 · The arrival
- **PICTURE:** Annville's Main Street with lights strung across it. Then the house: an inflatable snowman lies half-deflated next to a fallen ladder. The front door opens and lets snow in. Linda in the recliner with her walking boot propped up, surrounded by neighbors' casseroles. Jess in the doorway, coat still on, bag in hand.
- **SOUND:** The storm door bangs. The TV is on low. The piano holds.
- **CARD:** ANNVILLE, PENNSYLVANIA *(small serif, lower third, over Main Street)*
- **DIALOGUE:**
  - LINDA *(lighting up, completely casual)*: "You came!"
  - JESS *(breathless, four hours of driving in her voice)*: "You were in an *ambulance*."
  - LINDA *(reasonable)*: "They offered."
- **PICTURE:** Jess kneels by the boot. Linda's hand goes to her cheek. No words.
- **MUSIC:** The theme's first full statement: four notes, the fifth held back.

## STAGE 2: She tries to leave and gets stuck

### 0:20–0:26 · Leaving
- **PICTURE:** Turkey Hill at dawn under sodium lights, slush on the ground, the roller bag in the Tesla's back seat. Jess backs out with a coffee and her phone. Then the Tesla's bumper against the yellow plow blade of an old pickup. Then Dot in a blaze-orange cap, coffee in hand by the door, unmoved.
- **SOUND:** The store door chimes and the music cuts out. A tinny carol on the store speaker. The Tesla's soft low-speed hum.
- **DIALOGUE:**
  - JESS *(on the phone, brisk, already gone in her head)*: "Mom's fine. I'm back by noon—"
  - **CRUNCH:** the bumper plastic cracks, the steel blade gives a hollow *tonk*, a little ice falls. Then one full second of nothing but the store carol.
  - DOT *(appraising, not angry)*: "…Well. That's a Tesla."

### 0:26–0:34 · The garage
- **PICTURE:** Kreider's Auto: one bay, an orange glow from the space heater. Ben's boots stick out from under the Tesla. Reaction stills: Jess in a city coat too thin for the weather; Ben wiping his hands, not smiling yet.
- **SOUND:** A burst from an impact wrench is the downbeat. The comedy cue starts: upright bass, brushed snare, celesta, with the shop radio low underneath. The creeper's wheels roll out.
- **DIALOGUE:**
  - BEN *(rolls out, sees her, tiny pause)*: "…Hey, Jess."
  - JESS *(too bright, too quick)*: "Hi! Hi. Sorry—do I…?"
  - BEN *(lets it sit a full beat; flat)*: "Chemistry. You sat behind me."
  - JESS *(caught)*: "…Ben."
  - BEN *(amused, only slightly wounded)*: "You said 'do I' like it was a real question."

### 0:34–0:39 · Stuck
- **PICTURE:** Ben holds a cracked bumper sensor up to the light. Then the roller bag back on Jess's childhood bed, under a high-school pennant.
- **DIALOGUE:**
  - BEN: "Sensor's cracked. Comes from Tesla."
  - JESS *(urgent)*: "When?"
  - BEN: "Twenty-sixth."
  - JESS: "Of *December?*"
  - BEN *(beat, gently)*: "…That's the next one."
- **SOUND:** The comedy cue resolves on a pluck of the bass.

### 0:39–0:42 · Days pass
- **PICTURE:** Three quick stills of the same garage doorway. Across them her coat changes, the snow gets deeper, and she goes from holding one coffee to two.
- **DIALOGUE:**
  - JESS *(leaning in the doorway, by rote)*: "Part?"
  - BEN *(doesn't look up)*: "Nope."
- **SOUND:** One celesta note on each still.

## STAGE 3: She chooses to stay

### 0:42–0:51 · The turn
- **PICTURE:** Ben's truck on a farm lane at the edge of town, facing a wide field and the low sun. Shot from behind through the rear window: two silhouettes, a bench seat of space between them. Jess in profile, lit orange. Insert: her phone on the dash lights up **TYLER**. Very slow push in, a few drifting flakes.
- **SOUND:** Music drops to one held string note. Engine idle, heater fan, crows far off.
- **DIALOGUE:**
  - JESS *(quiet but voiced, not whispered; half a laugh at herself)*: "In the city, the sun goes behind a building at four. It doesn't set. It's just… over."
  - **SFX:** The phone buzzes on the dash. Her hand turns it face-down. The buzzing stops.
  - **SFX:** Ben turns the key and the engine stops. The silence opens up: wind, the engine block ticking.
  - BEN *(low, simple)*: "Give it a minute."
- **MUSIC:** The theme returns, four notes. The fifth is still held back.

### 0:51–0:56 · A hint of rupture
- **PICTURE:** Jess in the garage office doorway with a cardboard tray of two coffees. Her face. Ben turning around, caught.
- **SOUND:** Music out, hard. Office room tone, the space heater ticking.
- **DIALOGUE:**
  - BEN *(off-screen, to his uncle, matter-of-fact)*: "Looks great. Costs a fortune. Gets cold, shuts down."
  - **SFX:** The cardboard tray crunches.
  - JESS *(very level)*: "Shuts down."
  - BEN *(half a second too late)*: "…The car."

### 0:56–1:00 · The countdown
- **PICTURE:** Annville's town square at night: crowd, breath steam, kids on shoulders, the tree still dark. Jess alone in the frame, scanning the crowd. Ben's truck parked at the edge, empty.
- **SOUND:** A community brass band plays a phrase of "O Tannenbaum." Strings build with the count.
- **DIALOGUE:**
  - PA VOLUNTEER *(mic too close, civic cheer)*: "Okay, folks—couples up front!"
  - CROWD: "FIVE! FOUR! THREE! TWO—"

### 1:00–1:01 · Black
- Hard cut on "TWO—". One full second of total silence, longer than is comfortable.

### 1:01–1:04 · Title
- **PICTURE:** CHRISTMAS, FACING WEST over a field at the last edge of sunset, horizon low in the frame.
- **SOUND:** A single piano chord on the theme's fourth note, held. Still unresolved.

### 1:04–1:08 · The button
- **PICTURE:** Linda in the recliner, landline cord around one finger, eating pie straight from the tin.
- **DIALOGUE:**
  - LINDA *(low, conspiratorial, delighted)*: "Dot, the car's been ready since Tuesday." *(beat)* "Oh, she knows."

### 1:08–1:11 · Lights
- **SOUND:** The heavy *clunk* of the tree lights switching on. The crowd goes "Ohhh!" and cheers. Sleigh bells. The theme finally plays its fifth note and resolves with the full orchestra.
- **PICTURE:** Black blooms into warm, out-of-focus tree lights. We never see the tree or the couple.
- **CARD:** COMING THIS CHRISTMAS

## Notes

**Length:** About 71 seconds. To reach 70, tighten the title hold or the countdown by half a second each in the edit.

**Dialogue:** About 145 words, near the ceiling. The cold open is the most crowded section: Linda's "No, I know" has to come in close behind Jess's line. If auditions run long, cut in this order:
1. Linda's "They offered." Keep the hand on the cheek.
2. "If I leave" from the cold open.
3. Ben's "Sensor's cracked." Go straight to "When?"

**Payoffs to protect when cutting:**
- "Christmas" in the first line pays off in the final card.
- Tyler appears three times: the cost of leaving, the deal, the call she turns face-down.
- "Two days" and "back by noon" set up "Of *December?*"
- "Part?" "Nope." sets up "Oh, she knows."
- The fifth note of the theme is held back until the last card.

**Production:**
- **Music:** Use an original score, and record "O Tannenbaum" fresh (the composition is public domain).
- **Signage:** Keep Turkey Hill signs soft-focus unless you get clearance.
- **Voices:** Record every line on its own track, and build pauses and overlaps in the edit rather than in the voice model.
